TGS PLATFORM — Fix booking drawer clipping / can't minimise

FILE (overwrite): app/globals.css

THE BUG
  The booking drawer was positioned at top:0 with z-index:70, but the sticky
  site header is z-index:100. So the drawer's header — the "Your booking" title
  with the maximise and MINIMISE (–) controls — rendered BEHIND the site header
  and couldn't be seen or clicked. That's why it wouldn't minimise, and why it
  looked cut off at the top.

THE FIX (one rule, .bc-drawer)
  top: 0            → top: 76px               (drops below the 76px header)
  height: 100vh     → height: calc(100dvh - 76px)   (fits the space below it,
                                                      dvh so mobile browser
                                                      chrome doesn't clip the foot)
  The drawer head, controls and footer are now all visible; minimise works.

Note: the bottom-right "Booking" pill is the drawer's MINIMISED state, not a
stray button. Now that minimise is reachable again, clicking – returns to it.
The larger Luxury-Escapes-style booking flow is a separate future piece.

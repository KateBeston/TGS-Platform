** DATABASE ALREADY LIVE **: published_venue_services view now exposes date_mode

WHAT CHANGED — the sidebar calendar now adapts:
  • Single-date venue/service  -> ONE date picker labelled "Date" (+ a "Time"
    of day select when the service needs a time). No phantom "Departure".
  • Multi-day (stay / retreat)  -> Arrival + Departure as before.
  Departure tracks Arrival in single mode, so per-session pricing (nights || 1)
  is used and the slice records the day + chosen time.

HOW THE MODE IS DECIDED (per your confirmed model):
  • RetreatVenue  -> always "range" (retreats are multi-day).
  • WellnessVenue -> "range" if it has rooms OR any date_range service;
                     otherwise "single" (+ time if any service needs a time).
  Services inherit the venue-level date (pinned within the stay), matching what
  we agreed — no per-service pickers.

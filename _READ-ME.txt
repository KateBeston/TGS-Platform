THE FIX
  The room card has `overflow: hidden` and a hover `transform`. The modal was a
  DOM child of the card, so it was being clipped by the card (sitting off,
  cut edges) and trapped by the hover transform (the flickering on hover), and
  never showed as a full-screen card.
  RoomDetails now renders the modal through a React portal to document.body, so
  it escapes the card and opens as a true centred full-screen overlay. Nothing
  else changed.

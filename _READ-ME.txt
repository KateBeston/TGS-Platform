WHAT CHANGED (components/venue/BookingCart.tsx + globals.css)

  • A clean FLOATING BOX that tucks away to a small "Your booking" trigger
    bottom-right, and slides in when tapped (–, tuck away / ⤢, expand). It sits
    below the header, inset from the edges, as a bordered card with a shadow —
    never overlapping the hero, never clipped. Your page layout is untouched
    (full-width bands, hero, tabs all exactly as before).

    extras — each line with a stepper and price, then the estimate and actions.

    allowBuyout; WellnessVenue does not, so wellness venues never show it.

Nothing here touches the database or money. The "Review booking" button is
still inert — it becomes the hand-off to the cart page in the next stage.

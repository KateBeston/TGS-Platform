TGS PLATFORM — Experience booking control (booking process, piece 1 of 3)
=========================================================================
Drop over matching files. New: components/venue/ExperienceBooking.tsx.

WHAT THIS DOES
  The experience detail page (/experiences/[slug]) had a booking PLACEHOLDER.
  It now has a real request-to-book control in the sidebar:
    • preferred date, time of day, guests (stepper), estimated total
    • "Add to booking" adds the experience to the SAME holistic cart as rooms,
      filed under the experience's own venue (/{marketplace}/{listing_slug}),
      so an experience sits beside that venue's rooms in one booking
    • confirmation state with "Review booking" (to /booking) and "Add another time"
    • fires the cart-event tracking you already capture

WHY REQUEST-TO-BOOK: there are 0 published session slots, 0 instant-book venues,
and payments are gated — so the honest, launchable model is: assemble a booking,
send it as a request, the venue confirms. When venues later publish live
sessions, this control gains a slot picker; the cart shape does not change.
